# Savings Challenge & Budget

**Savings Challenge & Budget is an expense tracker and savings goals in a cozy game: an expense in a second, no ads and no banks, works offline, data only in your iCloud. Type the amount and tap a category, or snap a paper receipt; see what you can spend today, and save toward your own goals with 52-week, 100-envelope or custom-amount challenges while a hamster grows his burrow. Keep one budget with your partner through iCloud. Made for iPhone, including iPhone Duo. Free to download; the budget opens with a subscription.**

## How it works

1. **An expense in a second.** Tap the expense button, type the amount on the iPhone keypad and tap a category: the tile itself saves it. Paying with a paper receipt? Point the camera at it, and the total, date and shop are read on your phone. The hamster scoops grain from the day's heap into a basket.
2. **What you can spend today.** The line at the top shows today's allowance and what is left for the month, worked out from your budget, your expenses and the recurring payments still to come. Over budget? The heap is simply empty: no scolding and no "you overspent" notifications.
3. **Piggy bank.** Make savings goals (a trip, a safety cushion, a gift, a gadget, a home, studies or your own), note what you put aside and see when you will get there. Each goal fills a jar in its own room of the burrow. Challenges (52 weeks, 100 envelopes, no-spend days or your own amounts) are cells you tick in any order.
4. **Month.** See where the money went by category, compare with the same days of last month, search every entry, and fix or add an entry in any month, even a past one. Each month digs a new row of the burrow.
5. **Recurring.** Enter rent, salary or subscriptions once in Settings, and they are added on their day by themselves.

## Who it is for

People who want to see where their money goes and save for something real (a trip, a cushion, a gift) without connecting a bank; anyone who notes expenses at the till or in the evening and wants it done in a second; couples who keep one budget together; travellers who pay in several currencies; and anyone tired of budget apps full of ads.

## Questions and answers

**Is there a budget app without ads?** Yes, this one. There are no ads at all, no in-app currency and nothing to buy besides one subscription. The only outside code in the app is TelemetryDeck's anonymous usage statistics, which never see your amounts, categories or notes.

**Can I track expenses without connecting my bank?** Yes. The app never asks for a bank login and never holds or moves money. You add an expense in a second or snap a paper receipt, recurring payments are added on their day, and if you like, an automation you set up in Apple's Shortcuts app adds each in-store Apple Pay payment by itself.

**Can I set my own savings challenge amounts?** Yes. Start a 52-week challenge, 100 envelopes, a no-spend challenge or one with your own amounts. Change a cell's amount before you tick it, tick cells in any order, and skip a week without losing your progress.

**Can we keep a budget together as a couple?** Yes. Share one budget with one person through iCloud sharing, with no sign-ups: each entry shows who added it, and only the two of you can see it. Your own budget is never shared, and the person you invite adds entries there for free while your subscription is active.

**Does it work offline with several currencies?** Yes. Entries, today's allowance, the month and the piggy bank work without internet and without an account. An expense in another currency takes that day's official reference rate, which needs internet once for the day; offline, the app uses the last known rate and marks it, and you can always type your own rate.

**How much does it cost?** The download is free, and the budget opens with a subscription: 3 months for $24.99 or 1 week for $14.99. There is no free trial; if your Apple Account is eligible, the first week may be offered for $0.99, then $14.99 a week. No ads, nothing else to buy, Family Sharing included. Prices may vary by country.

**Who can see my money records?** Only you, and in a shared budget the person you invited. Records live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, never on our servers. Receipt photos are read on the phone and deleted right away.

**Which devices does it work on?** iPhone with iOS 26 or later, including iPhone Duo on both screens, in any orientation and in Split View. Widgets show what you can spend today without opening the app.

## Support

Questions, a problem or an idea? Write to **7tam.official@gmail.com**. We answer every
message, usually within two days.

- **Subscription:** manage or cancel it in iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, or in
  the App under Settings > Subscription.
- **Restore a purchase** on a new phone or after reinstalling: Restore in the App's Settings,
  or Restore on the subscription screen.
- **Without a subscription** your records stay: you can see your days, months, goals and
  reports and export your expenses and income as CSV in Settings.
- **Deleted something by mistake?** It waits 30 days in Settings > Recently Deleted.
- **Apple Pay entries** come from an automation you set up yourself in the Shortcuts app. It
  works for Apple Pay payments in shops, not online, and you can turn it off in Shortcuts at
  any time.
- **Your budget** stays on your device and in your own iCloud account. We cannot see it.
